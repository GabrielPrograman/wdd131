Core Principles of Design

Visual Hierarchy

Visual hierarchy is the arrangement of elements in a design to communicate the importance and relationships between different pieces of information.

Proximity Principle

The proximity principle states that related items should be grouped together to create a visual connection between them. This helps users understand the structure and organization of the content.

Key Design Elements

  • Contrast and Color
  • Alignment and Balance
  • Repetition and Consistency
  • White Space / Negative Space
